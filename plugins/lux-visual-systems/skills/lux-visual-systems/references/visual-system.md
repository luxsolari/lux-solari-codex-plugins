# Lux Solari visual system

## Identity

Combine four controlled languages:

- **Swiss / International editorial:** modular grid, typographic hierarchy, asymmetric balance, strong margins, precise alignment, thin rules, registration marks, and deliberate negative space.
- **Anime character language:** elegant silhouettes, expressive but controlled faces, clean anatomy, selective softness, and cinematic presence; never generic anime rendering.
- **Analogue photography and print:** 35mm sensibility, paper grain, film grain, halftone, restrained print imperfection, monochrome photography, and tactile cream stock.
- **Technical graphics:** diagrams, coordinates, optics, camera and film language, terminal fragments, instruments, labels, symbols, and functional metadata.

The result is quiet, structured, expressive, human, production-ready, and recognizably Lux Solari. Discipline creates freedom. Same tools, brighter worlds.

## Canonical palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Typical ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Primary field | Cream | `#F6F3EA` | 70% |
| Primary structure | Charcoal | `#0D0D0D` | 20% |
| Main accent | Signal Red | `#D62828` | 7% |
| Secondary accent | Mint | `#5B8F7D` | 3% |
| Neutral support | Warm Gray | `#C9C4B8` | flexible |

Keep accents sparse and purposeful. Preserve cream and charcoal dominance. A subject may require a small factual color exception, but it does not replace the system palette. Avoid neon, rainbow palettes, glossy gradients, and uncontrolled saturation.

## Typography and grid

- Sans: Inter or Helvetica Neue, bold/medium.
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono, regular/medium.
- Use small caps, generous tracking, compact technical labels, and minimal copy.
- Build on a modular grid with clear hierarchy, strong margins, asymmetric balance, crops, frames, thin rules, crosshairs, and registration marks.
- Text is structural, not decoration. Keep it short enough to render cleanly. Never fill empty space merely because it exists.

## Severity

Use these 1–10 targets as a balance, not as visible labels:

| Property | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Structural severity | 7 |
| Minimalism | 8 |
| Decoration | 2 |
| Text density | 2 |
| Industrial influence | 6 |
| Anime softness | 5 |
| Film texture | 4 |
| Cyberpunk | 1 |
| Dystopia | 1 |

Texture is analogue, subtle, and tactile: light paper grain, film grain, halftone, and restrained print imperfection. Avoid clutter, noisy overlays, heavy distressing, excessive text, unrelated branding, generic cyberpunk cliches, and ornament without purpose.
